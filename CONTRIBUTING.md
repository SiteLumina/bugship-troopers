# Contributing

Until the 0.1 extraction lands, contributions are by invitation. After that:

- Every change to a guard, a lane, or a reviewer brief lands with the proof that the guard fails without it, written in the commit message.
- No guard is weakened without a ledger row explaining why and a fresh mutation proof.
- No secrets, no internal hostnames, no customer data, anywhere in this repository. The secret-scan hook runs on every commit.
- Commits are authored by the contributor. Maintained by Site Lumina.
